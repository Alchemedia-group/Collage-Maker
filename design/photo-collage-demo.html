Store listing: Photo Collage | Maker & Editor

Design preview. Hero photos are from Unsplash. This runs in your browser as the design reference for the Android app, which is built natively in Kotlin to match it. Saving writes to History only, and Pro is a switch, not a payment.

Try this

  • Tap All in any layout row to browse, search or filter by photo count. Try “heart”, “love” or “5”.
  • Under Letters, type your own word to make a layout.
  • Tap Design your own layout in the Home hero, or in the layout browser, to draw cells, pick shapes and save them to My layouts. Cells can run past the canvas edge, and shapes grow to 300%.
  • The clock on Home opens History: every collage you save, ready to edit again. Delete anything with the bin or the ⋯ menu, and tap Undo if you change your mind.
  • Open Shape for social sizes (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, A4 print), and Style › Frame for polaroid, film, stamp, torn paper, tape and lift.
  • Select a photo, then Rotate to straighten, turn and mirror it, or Light for its own brightness, contrast and saturation.
  • For text, try Color › Gradient, the new Style presets, and the star on a font to keep it under Favourites.
  • Save, then pick an app under Share to.
  • Add Text and Stickers: pick a font, colour and style, then drag, pinch or use the corner handle to resize and rotate.
  • In the editor, tap a cell, then drag to move its photo and scroll or pinch to zoom, up to 20×.
  • Under Style › Image, put one of your pictures behind the photos.
  • Tap a tool again to collapse its panel and get more canvas.
  • Save under Free and Pro to compare the watermark.
